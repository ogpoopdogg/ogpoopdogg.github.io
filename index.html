Staff tools

Good day

Every E&C app in one place. Pick one to open it, or press its key.

  • CustomersP Request Pickup Customers book pickups and deliveries, save their contacts, and follow each request to dispatch, with a history and printable statements. /requestpickup Open
  • DispatchH Route Helper Build multi-stop routes of pickups and deliveries, auto-optimize the stop order, and save or share them with drivers. /routehelper Open
  • DispatchW RouteWatcher The dispatch dashboard: watch every driver live on the map from their GPS trackers, review their tracks, and compare them to the planned route. /RouteWatcher Open
  • DispatchC Calculator Price a shipment with the courier rate calculator, compare actual against dimensional weight box by box, and keep a log history of each calculation. /calculator Open
  • StaffT Time Clock Employees punch in and out and pick their vehicle for the day. The admin panel shows who's working, edits punches, sets schedules, and exports timesheets. /timeclock/admin Open
  • FleetG Garage One page for every vehicle: parts and bulb sizes, fluids and tires, problems drivers report, oil checks, and the check schedule. /garage Open
  • InternalN Notes Portal The internal workspace for technical logs, system updates, and team notes. /developernotes Open
